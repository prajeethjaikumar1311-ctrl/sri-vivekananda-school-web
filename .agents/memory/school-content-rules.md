---
name: School content rules
description: Content and image sourcing rules for the official school website.
---

Use only school facts and imagery provided or verified in official materials. Do not use stock, generated, or unrelated images. Do not add unsupported statistics, awards, facilities, board affiliations, fees, school hours, bus routes, testimonials, or current announcements. Dates shown on posters are archival unless the school confirms they are current. Public-facing copy should use “Sri Vivekananda School, Singarapettai” and omit “Nursery” and “Primary.”

**Why:** The user requested the consistent public-facing school name “Sri Vivekananda School, Singarapettai” and asked to remove “Nursery” and “Primary” everywhere.

**How to apply:** Use “Sri Vivekananda School, Singarapettai” in site copy and metadata. Before adding content or images, verify them against supplied school materials; label poster dates as archived/reference unless the user confirms they apply now.
