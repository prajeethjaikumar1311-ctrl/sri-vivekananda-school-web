---
name: School content rules
description: Content and image sourcing rules for the official school website.
---

Use only school facts and imagery provided or verified in official materials. Do not use stock, generated, or unrelated images. Do not add unsupported statistics, awards, facilities, board affiliations, fees, school hours, bus routes, testimonials, or current announcements. Dates shown on posters are archival unless the school confirms they are current. Preserve the school's official name and spelling.

**Why:** The user explicitly required authentic school materials and no invented claims; the supplied posters include dates that may no longer be current.

**How to apply:** Before adding content or images, verify them against supplied school materials. Label poster dates as archived/reference unless the user confirms they apply now.
